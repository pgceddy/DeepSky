No star-hopping, no guessing what's up. Scan the patch of sky your Seestar can actually see, and let the app do the sorting.

01

Point

Hold your phone or iPad toward the sky. Full 3D orientation tracking reads your exact aim — steady even near the zenith, where ordinary compass apps lose their footing.

02

Scan

Every object within your field of view appears instantly, pulled from the full OpenNGC catalog with an offline fallback for nights without signal.

Targets are ranked by altitude trajectory, brightness, and Seestar frame fit — so the top of the list is genuinely worth your imaging time.

Built around a real imaging session

Ascending / descending badges

See at a glance whether a target is climbing toward better altitude or already past its peak for the night.

Frame-fit scoring

Objects are checked against the Seestar S50's actual field of view, so you're not left with a galaxy that spills off the edge.

Planning mode

Drag the time slider to preview the sky at any hour of the night and plan your session before you ever go outside.

Per-object imaging notes

Integration-time guidance by magnitude, edge-on galaxy detection, and nearby-companion flags for objects worth a wider frame.

Integration-time payoff curve

See exactly where added exposure stops paying off — a live SNR curve for the object you're viewing, so you know when to stop and move on.

Everything in one card

Rise time, best imaging window, a Wikipedia summary, and processing tips for that object type — all surfaced together, no separate tabs or searches.
